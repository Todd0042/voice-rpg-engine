# Project Overview: The Squeeze (Working Title)
## Genre & Format
- **Platform:** Android (Optimized for portrait-mode, touch controls, quick-session play).
- **Engine/AI Target:** Structured narrative RPG / interactive memoir.
- **Tone:** Unflinching, gritty, realistic, psychological, and tragic. It is not a power-fantasy; it is a story of institutional attrition, survival, and moral integrity.
- **Core Theme:** The repeating corporate lifecycle ("Honeymoon, Drift, Squeeze") and the physical/mental toll of retail management across multiple U.S. states.

## Key Mechanics & Narrative Rules
1. **No Hollywood Endings:** The protagonist does not beat the system or save the corporation. Victory is defined by surviving with one's sanity, empathy for associates, and life intact.
2. **Realism First:** Metrics, management titles (IMS2, CSM, Support Manager, ACCM, Overnight Stocking Coach), and corporate mechanisms (Open Door, Sedgwick leave) must reflect authentic, real-world retail operations.
3. **The Relocation Debt Trap:** Corporate relocations come with the hanging sword of a relocation benefit contract (creating financial "doom" and debt leverage if terminated). *Exception:* The move from Lamar, Colorado to South Carolina was an independent relocation paid for entirely out of pocket by the protagonist to maintain autonomy.
4. **The Shingles Motif:** Shingles flare-ups act as the body's physical scorecard for corporate stress, recurring across Acts 2, 3, 4, and 5.
5. **Character Consistency:** Maintain the specific interpersonal dynamics, regional management styles, and altered-yet-recognizable character names provided in the script.
